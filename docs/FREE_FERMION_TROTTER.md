# Free-fermion check of the Trotter coefficient versus lattice size

The Hubbard step counts use a doublon coefficient C = |D_r − D_exact| r² measured at U/J = 4 on
3×3, 4×3, and 4×4 patches. The termwise value is not converged in patch size (0.034 on 3×3, 0.19
on 4×4 at τ = 1), which raises the question of whether it keeps growing with L. At U = 0 the question
can be answered exactly at any size.

## Method (`python/free_fermion_trotter.py`)

At U = 0 every gate of the termwise and plaquette circuits, including the fermionic Hadamards and
the Jordan–Wigner parity strings, is a rotation generated by a Majorana bilinear. The circuits are
therefore Gaussian and run exactly on the 2n×2n Majorana covariance matrix, and the doublon at the
central site follows from Wick's theorem. The operations are those of
`hubbard_circuits.build_workload` (identical for even L; odd L uses the same construction), from the
Néel state. The Gaussian simulation agrees with a state vector on 2×2 and 3×3 to 10⁻¹⁴, and C is
second order (r = 16 and 32 agree to a few percent). Results:
[results/free_fermion_trotter_v1/coefficients.json](../results/free_fermion_trotter_v1/coefficients.json).

## Result (r = 32)

| L | 3 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 16 | 20 | 24 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| termwise, τ = 1 | 0.15 | 0.024 | 0.017 | 0.017 | 0.017 | 0.016 | 0.016 | 0.016 | 0.016 |
| termwise, τ = 2 | 1.42 | 0.36 | 0.12 | 0.000 | 0.022 | 0.020 | 0.021 | 0.021 | 0.021 |
| termwise, τ = 4 | 0.99 | 3.9 | 2.3 | 2.5 | 0.83 | 0.005 | 0.26 | 0.077 | 0.067 |
| plaquette, τ = 1 | 0.016 | 0.011 | 0.001 | 0.004 | 0.004 | 0.004 | 0.004 | 0.004 | 0.004 |
| plaquette, τ = 4 | 0.11 | 0.20 | 0.087 | 0.52 | 0.031 | 0.000 | 0.005 | 0.005 | 0.003 |

At fixed τ the central coefficient becomes independent of L once the lattice is wider than the
light cone, L ≳ 4τ + 2; beyond that the Jordan–Wigner ordering of the termwise circuit adds no size
dependence. Inside the light cone, small patches fluctuate by up to two orders of magnitude and
mostly overestimate the bulk value: at τ = 4 the 4×4 patch gives 3.9 against a bulk 0.07. The
largest coefficient over all sites, which includes boundary sites, converges the same way (0.44 at
τ = 2, 0.96 at τ = 4, termwise). On the time-growing family τ = L/4 the central coefficient stays
below 0.5 for every L ≤ 24 and does not grow systematically, while the planning coefficients used
at U/J = 4 are 0.21 (L = 4) to 13 (L = 24).

## Scope

This checks hopping-only Trotter error and Jordan–Wigner ordering. The interaction commutators that
dominate at U/J = 4 are absent, so the U = 0 values are 10–100× smaller than the calibrated ones and
do not replace them. What carries over is the structure: in the free limit the coefficient is set
by the light cone, not by L, and finite patches err on the high side. The 3×3 → 4×4 growth of the
termwise coefficient at U/J = 4 (the opposite direction to U = 0) is therefore unlikely to be a hopping or ordering artifact, and exact interacting
strips confirm it: at τ = 1 the termwise coefficient converges to 0.19 by length 5
([STRIP_DYNAMICS.md](STRIP_DYNAMICS.md)).
