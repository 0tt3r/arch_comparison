# Surgery calibration contract

This is a new circuit-level calibration of isolated rotated-patch gadgets, not a routing or congestion model. Code and inputs are frozen in `input_sources/`; `manifest.json` fixes every cell, seed, shot count, batch size, and version. Pilot and PBS smoke shots are excluded from `raw.csv`.

## Geometry, measurement, and timing

Data lie at odd/odd coordinates and checks at even/even coordinates. Rotating these coordinates by 45 degrees gives an ordinary nearest-neighbor square interaction graph. X checks occupy the top/bottom boundaries and Z checks the left/right boundaries. Logical X is a vertical column; logical Z is a horizontal row. Each extraction round has the same four hook-safe dressed-CZ slots as Stim's rotated memory circuit, followed by measurement and reset. `schedule_validation.json` checks every CZ slot and every noisy gate location against `surface_law_calibration.py` for d=3,5,7,9. Hadamard duration is fused into the slots, exactly as in that reference.

ZZ surgery joins vertically separated patches. Fresh gap data are prepared in X, the merged rectangle is stabilized for R_m rounds, and X readout of the gap splits it. XX surgery is the transposed construction with Z gap preparation/readout. The CNOT places C above ancilla A and T to its right: initialize A in logical |+>, measure Z_C Z_A, measure X_A X_T, then destructively read out Z_A. The usual logical frame is Z_C raised to the XX result and X_T raised to the XOR of the ZZ and ancilla-Z results; the implementation derives the complete frame, including split-boundary signs, from exact Stim stabilizer flows. The four Bell-reference output constraints verify the intended CNOT, not just deterministic syndromes. This implements the merge/split principle of [Horsman et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.4022) and the Pauli-measurement accounting of [Litinski](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.02892).

R_pre=R_post=d are noisy storage windows around each experiment. The CNOT contains two consecutive R_m=d merges and no intervening full storage window. Split readout takes an explicitly additional 100 ns, with DEPOLARIZE1(p) on retained data; the CNOT includes two splits and one ancilla readout. Thus its merge/readout core counts 2d×240 ns+300 ns, conditional on prestaging fresh bridge/check resets in a preceding available slot. Their reset errors are present, but a separate preparation latency is not charged. If hardware cannot overlap these resets, add that latency and corresponding retained-data idle noise; the stated noise conventions do not settle this scheduling choice. The 4d extraction rounds of the complete probe also include the pre/post storage windows. Decoder latency and physical initial encoding time are not modeled; frame tracking does not execute additional noisy Pauli gates.

For l=1 or 3 the gap has l*d data columns, spanning one patch width in the transverse direction. **l=0 means the minimal one-data-column seam, not a nonexistent physical gap.** Its active area adds R_m/d to the nominal two-patch tile-round volume. Active area-equivalent volume is V=4d+(2+max(1,l*d)/d)R_m for parity and V=5d+6R_m+2R_m/d for CNOT. Independent-patch parity controls use V=4d+(2+l)R_m. Additional `cnot_memory` controls store three independent reference-entangled patches before and during the two merges, and two afterwards, giving V=5d+6R_m. The `cnot_core` experiment uses the identical complete CNOT probe but removes noise from its pre/post storage windows; its active noisy volume is V=6R_m+2R_m/d. At R_m=d these are 11d, 6d+2, and 11d+2 for matched memory, isolated CNOT core, and full CNOT probe, respectively. CSV also records nominal tile volume and a duration-equivalent volume including the additional split idles. Those variants should not be silently interchanged.

## Probe boundary and error definitions

**Data inputs are ideally encoded halves of logical Bell pairs with noiseless external reference qubits.** The ancilla input is ideally encoded |+>. An ideal initial extraction establishes all stabilizer signs before the noisy pre-window. Terminal perfect local-check and commuting logical-Pauli measurements close the output boundary. These nonlocal ideal reference operations are a channel-test harness, not an implementation credit. Without a protected encoded input, the probe measures unprotected encoding errors instead of the surgery channel. Fresh bridge preparation, syndrome extraction, split readout, and CNOT ancilla readout remain noisy.

For ZZ surgery, the four decoded reference checks are: reported parity versus Z_refA Z_refB; Z_A Z_refA; Z_B Z_refB; and X_A X_B X_refA X_refB. `parity` records any wrong reported parity. `space` is the union of the last three checks; its first two resolve bit errors on either patch, while the phase check resolves their joint phase sector. `time_only` means **wrong reported parity with no detected data-channel error**, not a microscopic fault-location label. Spatial fault mechanisms can also enter this event. `total` is their union; equivalently q_total=q_space+q_time_only. It is not q_space+q_parity, which double-counts their intersection. Pauli patterns in `histogram` preserve all correlations. The analogous four CNOT checks compare outputs with the CNOT-transformed reference stabilizers, giving a total stochastic channel-failure probability rather than a sum of separate X/Z experiments.

Local syndrome detectors are built by exact CSS stabilizer updates, rebased onto local checks after every round. At a split, logical parity is excluded from the detector set: using a known logical input as a detector would artificially repair time-like failures. All four observable flows are solved against the same pre-readout circuit, so none can use another ideal logical readout to repair itself. Every production cell checks noiseless determinism, a deliberately inserted logical X fault, graph decomposition, shortest graphlike fault distance, and every single undecomposed detector-error-model mechanism against MWPM (except the intentionally distance-one R_m=1 boundary). First-round detectors compare against the pre-round local basis; split detectors explicitly close checks supported entirely on the removed bridge. This locality is important for the graph decomposition used by ordinary MWPM. The latter is a circuit diagnostic for this decomposed detector model, not an independent exhaustive circuit-distance theorem.

## Controls and inference

Matched memory probes use independent Bell-referenced patches with the same active tile-round counts (and the same split idle on the two retained data patches). Their full Pauli failure can be compared directly with the gadget. Separate original product-preparation/destructive-readout memory controls call `surface_law_calibration.circuit` unchanged at R=10d in X and Z. These are compared both with the saved endpoint-corrected basis fit and with the original matching fixed-shot cells. The old fit already has known lack of fit; disagreement is reported, never forced to pass.

Sampling is fixed in advance, with ordinary PyMatching MWPM and correlations disabled. Each 25,000-shot batch has its own deterministic seed. Cell intervals are exact two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson intervals. Cells with fewer than 30 failures are flagged; zero failures supply an upper bound, not a measured zero rate. Reported ratio intervals condition on the saved memory-fit point estimates; their endpoints do not include uncertainty in the fit or the ideal probe boundary.

The temporal binomial fit uses q_time_only=B_(d,l) Lambda_t^[-(R_m+1)/2], with separate amplitudes for each (d,l), a shared slope at each p, and profile-likelihood intervals. A rejected fit is a descriptive slope, not a validated time-like law. Common gadget multipliers similarly fit q=1-exp[-c V p_L(d)] and retain a goodness-of-fit diagnostic. The first tested R_m where time-only error falls below space error is recorded both by point estimates and by separated pointwise intervals. No untested threshold is certified. This study excludes congestion, twists/Y surgery, correlated physical noise, non-Pauli leakage, real reference preparation/readout costs, and d>9.

## Retired diagnostic run

The first production run used a generic elimination basis at transitions. It preserved the stabilizer constraints but produced nonlocal detector hyperedges that ordinary MWPM misdecoded even for some single physical faults: 15 single DEM mechanisms at d=3,l=3,p=10^-4 and four at d=5. Rebasing onto local pre-round and split checks eliminates these failures without changing a single physical gate or noise location; `detector_basis_diagnostic.json` retains an explicit two-qubit Pauli-fault witness and the physical-circuit identity check. All 586 million shots of that earlier run are preserved under `retired_detector_basis/` and excluded from the final tables and fits. The corrected campaign has 158 fixed-shot cells and 678 million shots. Its manifest and input snapshots are independent of the retired campaign. Both jobs count against the requested node-hour budget.

## Fit interpretation

The companion fit_interpretation.json flags a sparse local temporal fit (p=10^-4,d=5,l=1) with failures only at its shortest merge. Its numerical slope reaches the imposed search cap, so it supplies a lower profile bound, not a finite measured suppression factor. A shared temporal slope is rejected at p=10^-3; per-geometry fits and per-cell intervals are retained. The original memory denominator sums X and Z logical-component rates and includes a separately fitted endpoint model for its original controls. It should not be mistaken for an exact finite-window any-Pauli probability.
